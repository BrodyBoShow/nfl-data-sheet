## Scope
- **Usage and role analyst** (`usage.py`): snap share (offense, defense, ST, for every
  player who takes a snap), target share, air-yards share, red-zone/goal-line/end-zone
  share, carry share, week-over-week deltas. Reads `player_week`/`snaps`. Writes
  `player_usage_week`.
- **Player efficiency analyst** (`player_efficiency.py`): per-player rate stats for
  receiving, rushing, passing, and defense. It's the individual-side counterpart to P2's
  team-level Efficiency analyst. Reads `player_week`, `ngs`, `pfr_advstats`, `snaps`,
  and the new `player_game_pbp` and `participation_player_season` staged tables. Writes
  `player_eff_week`.
- **Scheme analyst** (`scheme.py`), team-level, writes `signals`:
  - pass rate over expected, neutral pace
  - play-action/motion/RPO/screen rates, box counts, blitz/pressure rate
  - approximate personnel (labeled as approximate)
  - run defense by gap/direction, PFR nearest-defender coverage-proxy splits
  - participation-derived man/zone and coverage-shell tendencies, labeled as
    multi-season history (see "Participation labeling rule")
- **nflverse bulk collector, extended** (not a new collector: participation is the same
  nflverse-data release family, one collector per source family):
  - Widened `ngs`/`pfr_advstats`/`ftn` columns.
  - A player-keyed pbp+FTN count aggregation (`player_game_pbp`).
  - A participation player-season aggregation (`participation_player_season`, tag
    `pbp_participation`), gated by that tag's own `timestamp.json`.
- **Retention job** (L0, `pipeline/orchestration/retention.py`): the decided policies
  L2 and L4 below.
- **Intel collector**: ESPN NFL news feed, official team RSS where available, Sleeper
  trending players as a news-spike indicator. Dedupe by URL + text hash. Rule-based
  tagging only (keywords + crosswalk name matching): team, player_id, category (injury,
  depth chart, suspension, coaching, signing/release/trade). No LLM calls.

## Decisions (user, 2026-09-25)
1. **Shape:** wide per-analyst player tables. **Team-level signals stay in `signals`.**
   The single-shape contract still holds for everything the model reads; the exception
   is player detail only (CLAUDE.md layer rules, `docs/signals.md` "Player tables").
2. **Detail:** the full version. Every metric stores season-to-date (STD, prior-blended),
   this game, and last-4 games. The last-4 values answer "is this player trending."
3. **Retention:**
   - **L2 yes:** staged nflverse player tables keep only `[season-1, season]`.
   - **L4 yes:** after a season ends, its weekly player-table rows collapse to each
     player's final row.
   - **L5 deferred:** the `signals.inputs_version` lookup table.
   - **L3 not decided:** trimming `injuries.raw`. First find out whether the changelog's
     ~450 rows/day are real changes or hash churn (open item 1).
4. **Archive** (export completed seasons to Parquet, delete from Postgres): deferred.
   It's the plan for before the 2028 offseason; revisit then.
5. **Participation priors:** included, with CC-BY-SA 4.0 attribution, the same as FTN.

FTN-derived per-player fields inherit these denominators. Rush-by-gap spreads 17

### Coverage: who covered whom
**No free source says which defender covered which receiver on a snap.** That takes
tracking-derived matchup charting (PFF, SIS, NGS's non-public matchup product). The
closest proxies, and how each differs:
1. **PFR `def_targets`/allowed stats** (best in-season proxy): per defender per game,
   attributed by PFR's charting to the defender judged responsible on *targeted* plays.
   - No untargeted coverage snaps, so no real denominator.
   - No receiver identity: these are game aggregates.
   - Charting judgment, not tracking.
2. **pbp defender credits** (`pass_defense_*`, tackler, INT): play-level, and on the
   same row as `receiver_player_id`, so it is a real receiver↔defender link. But it only
   exists when that defender broke up, intercepted, or tackled, and a tackle isn't
   coverage. It's biased toward completions and PDs.
3. **Participation** (historical only): on the field isn't covering. It supports
   receiver-vs-man/zone/shell splits, and an on-field-dropbacks denominator for PFR
   targets, as priors only.
4. **NGS `avg_cushion`/`avg_separation`**: distance to the *nearest* defender, identity
   unknown, thresholded.

Any signal built on these is documented as what it is, never as a matchup or a
PFF-style grade.

### Participation labeling rule (decision carry-forward A)
Participation is 2016–2025, historical only, with man/zone on ~49% of plays. **Anything
derived from it is a multi-season tendency, not current-season behavior.**
- Signal and column names end in `_hist`.
- `inputs_version` names the seasons used (e.g. `pbp_participation@<ts>[2023-2025]`),
  and the registry entry states the span.
- The UI shows the span next to the value ("38% man, 2023–25"). "This defense plays 38%
  man" must never read as "this year."

## Storage design (research pass, 2026-09-25)

### Measured baseline
- `pg_database_size` 204.6 MB (195 MiB). `signals` is 74.5 MB of it (50.7 heap + 23.8
  index), 164,596 rows = **453 B/row**. `inputs_version` is 137 chars on efficiency
  rows; the unique index is 93 B/row.
- All-in growth today is **~139 MB/season**, not 27:
  - signals ~27
  - staged nflverse player tables ~29 (`ftn` 10.0, `player_week` 7.0, `snaps` 6.5,
    `pfr_advstats` 4.3, `ngs` 0.7). These never roll off: the collector fetches
    `[season-1, season]`, and nothing deletes older seasons.
  - `injuries` ~75 (open item 1; assumes ~168 in-season collection days)
  - other ~8
- Players who take ≥1 off/def snap in a 2025 week averaged **1,143**. The
  season-to-date "seen" population averaged **1,680** (1,166 in week 1, 1,965 by week
  18), a ratio of 1.47.
- Free plan: read-only at 500 MB *database size* (Supabase docs, "Free Plan behavior",
  read 2026-09-25). Pro: $25/mo, 8 GB (supabase.com/pricing, 2026-09-25).

### Row-cost model
- **`signals` row:** 453 B, measured.
- **Wide row (estimate; step 7 measures it):**
  - ~130 B fixed (keys, `as_of`, a 4-byte `inputs_version` id, `content_hash`), plus
    6 B per non-null metric value (4 B `real` + 2 B `smallint` league pct).
  - Multiply by 1.2 for page overhead and upsert churn, then add ~56 B of indexes.
  - A null costs 1 bit.
- **Season = 19 REG-week equivalents** (18 + postseason).

### Runway with the decided policies
Assumes the planned `injuries_backup_pre_changelog` drop (−33 MB, P6 §8 Q3), and P7
shipping ~2026 week 6 with a 2025 backfill (collapsed under L4 immediately).

| Scenario | End 2026 | Growth/season after | Crosses 500 MB |
|---|---|---|---|
| Status quo, no P7 | ~262 | 139 | ~Dec 2028 |
| P7 in `signals`, naive (rejected) | backfill alone 192 + 351 = 543 | ~490 | on the 2025 backfill run |

- The decided path crosses slightly *before* the status quo. L2 saves ~29 MB/season,
  but P7 adds a one-time ~58 MB in 2026 and ~19 MB/season permanently (scheme 16.5,

- Of the 129 MB/season, **injuries is 75**. Cutting injuries growth by ~40 MB/season

- Pro is only required for player detail in the `signals` shape, or for all history
  kept live without an archive.

### Retention policies (decided)
- **L2, staged player tables:** `player_week`, `snaps`, `ngs`, `ftn`, `pfr_advstats`,
  and `player_game_pbp` keep `season >= current_season - 1`.
  - Everything older is re-fetchable from nflverse, and backfills already refetch via
    `seasons_override`.
  - `team_week` is exempt (tiny; the backtest uses 2018+). So are `depth` (latest only)
    and `participation_player_season` (0.5 MB/season; it *is* the prior).
- **L4, player tables:** once a season is complete (its last game has a final score in
  `games`, read by the L0 job), delete every `player_usage_week`/`player_eff_week` row
  for that season except each player's latest-week row.
  - Team-level `signals` keep full weekly history (the backtest reads it).
  - Collapsed history is recomputable by refetching the season and re-running the
    analysts.
- Both run in the L0 retention job with a dry-run mode that reports rows and MB before
  deleting.

### Existing research findings (2026-09-22, still valid)
**Free and already staged, thin reads:** `player_week` EPA ÷ attempts/targets/carries;
`ngs.avg_yac_above_expectation`, `ngs.avg_separation`;
`pfr_advstats.rushing_yards_before_contact_avg`; `player_week` air yards.

**Free but not yet staged** (a migration change to an already-verified source):
- `ngs`: `avg_cushion`, `percent_share_of_intended_air_yards`, `avg_time_to_los`,
  `avg_intended_air_yards`, `avg_completed_air_yards`, and `avg_time_to_throw`.
- `pfr_advstats` `def`: `def_targets`, `def_completions_allowed`, `def_yards_allowed`,
  `def_completion_pct`, `def_adot`, `def_air_yards_completed`, `def_yards_after_catch`,
  `def_times_blitzed`, `def_times_hurried`, `def_times_hitqb`, `def_sacks`,
  `def_tackles_combined`, `def_missed_tackles`, `def_ints`, `def_receiving_td_allowed`,
  `def_yards_allowed_per_cmp`, `def_yards_allowed_per_tgt`. The `def` stat_type has no
  `position` column; join `snaps.position`.
- `ftn`: the 13 unstaged columns listed in the inventory.

**The load-bearing new engineering is the player-keyed pbp aggregation.**
- `player_week` has no success flag, and pbp's `run_location`/`run_gap` are never
  aggregated to a player grain.
- `player_game_pbp` (counts and sums, grouped by passer/rusher/receiver id, joined to
  FTN by `game_id`/`play_id`) supplies:
  - success rate
  - rush EPA/success by gap and direction
  - stuffed rate (`yards_gained <= 0`)
  - FTN-flag splits per player
- The team-defense-by-gap split is its team-level companion.

**Pressure rate / run-stop rate (unit-side):**
- Pressure rate: `def_pressures` over a pass-attempts-faced denominator.
- PFF-style per-defender run stops have **no free source**. PFR `def` is
  pass-defense-only. The honest substitute is team rush EPA/success by gap.

**Alignment (slot/perimeter) matchups are dropped from scope (decision, 2026-09-22).**
- No receiver-alignment or nearest-defender-role field exists in any free source.
- Where a directional split helps, use pbp's `pass_location`, labeled as a field-location
  split, never as slot/perimeter.
