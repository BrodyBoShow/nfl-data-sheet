## Scope
- Availability collector: ESPN injuries, Sleeper players (≤1/day). NFL.com injury report
  scraping stayed out of scope for this session. Stores `injuries` (append-only
  snapshot log, both sources) and `transactions` (team/designation changes, derived by
  diffing consecutive snapshots). No `practice_status` table — live verification found
  neither source exposes structured per-day participation data; see
  `docs/architecture.md` §Deviations and `docs/sources.md`'s Availability section.
- Availability impact analyst: snap-share redistribution, replacement depth-rank delta
  (depth-chart order only, not a quality estimate — see `docs/signals.md`), OL/secondary
  cluster counts, practice-trend risk (a designation-change ordinal, not real practice
  participation). Consumes raw snap shares (from the `snaps` table, populated by the
  Phase 2 nflverse bulk collector) as the redistribution baseline, since Usage/role's
  more refined target/carry shares aren't built until P7. Revisit whether to switch to
  Usage's shares once P7 exists.
- Source verification: ESPN + Sleeper endpoints live-called, fixtures saved,
  `docs/sources.md` updated from UNVERIFIED to VERIFIED with real shapes/limits.
- `player_id_crosswalk.sleeper_id` coverage gap found and partially fixed: `id_spine.py`
  now self-heals it (fill-null-only, from Sleeper's own self-reported `gsis_id`); the
  Availability collector stays read-only against the crosswalk.
- Two shared modules pulled out during this phase, not duplicated per-collector:
  `pipeline/core/team_aliases.py` (retired-franchise codes + ESPN's `WSH`/`WAS` quirk,
  also now used by `nflverse_bulk.py`) and `pipeline/core/schedule.py`
  (`resolve_season_week` — derives season/week from the `games` table for sources with no
  week of their own, rather than trusting a run's `ctx.week`).
