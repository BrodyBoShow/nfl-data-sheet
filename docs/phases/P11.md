# P11 — Readability

**Status: OPEN.** Scoped 2026-10-27 (local), approved the same night. Slices 1–3 are the
target for that session, and slice 3 is the stopping point.

**Goal:** a sheet that can be read and acted on during the season. The measured problem:
almost every number is bare.
- 76 efficiency matchup values with no league average, median or rank.
- A page of 25 tables, 236 columns and 4,901 cells.
- 14 distinct ways to interpret a number, and 4,416 characters of legend.
- The counts come from the scoping's own inventory of the live page. That script wasn't
  kept, so they aren't reproducible (CLAUDE.md, "A figure that reaches a public page…").
  They are planning figures, never published. Any before/after figure that reaches a
  page is re-measured by a kept script.

It's week 8 of 18. A redesign that lands in January is worth nothing this season, so the
work ships in slices, each usable on its own.

## Decisions (user, 2026-10-27 local)

1. **Architecture: a summary block at the top of `/game/`, the existing detail below,
   collapsed with `<details>`.** Not a rebuild, and not a separate route.
   - **Why not a separate route:** it duplicates every number, and the two copies drift.
   - **Why not a rebuild:** the honesty suite (tests 1–18) and most of the web tests are
     tied to the current page structure. `<details>` keeps collapsed content in the
     server-rendered HTML, so those assertions keep holding.
   - The summary renders from the same view-model functions as the detail (`familyRows`,
     `pctPopulation`, `UnitCells`). It never runs its own query.
2. **Headline efficiency matchup rows: seven.** EPA/play, EPA/play pass, EPA/play rush,
   success rate, explosive rate, points per drive, red-zone TD rate. The other 13 bases
   collapse.
   - **EPA/play stays even though pass + rush nearly derive it.** It's the model's only
     input, so a reader should see what the projection is built from. Don't optimize it
     away.
   - **Red-zone TD rate earns its slot:** it decides games, and none of the others
     derive it.
3. **Tendency: defense side only.** Blitz rate (r 0.66), inside-run share faced (0.48),
   middle-target share faced (0.41), all measured in P10.
   - **The offense side doesn't ship.** It has no measured persistence. Shipping an
     unmeasured signal is what the measurement rules exist to prevent.
   - If it's wanted later, measure it first. That's slice 6's problem.
4. **The five ways to read a number** (the scoping's section C) are verified against the
   live page, not mapped onto the 14 (that list isn't in the repo). Every distinct way a
   reader has to interpret a number falls into one of the five, or is a named,
   deliberate exception.
5. Everything else in the scoping is approved as written (below).

## Scope, as approved

### A. Reference points
- **Stored but not shown: player usage `_pct` (12), non-headline offense `_pct` (~90),
  and defense TFL/sacks/QB hits per snap `_pct`.**
  - The defense ones are ungated: 402 of 817 week-7 rows have them.
  - Not display-only: `web.player_usage` grants no `_pct` column and `web.player_eff`
    grants four. Widening is migration `0038` plus a `docs/phases/P6.md` §2 update.
    That's slice 5.
- **Computable into an existing column: team `league_pct`.** That's an Efficiency
  change. `fit_inputs_hash` hashes `(season, week, team, signal, value)` only, so there's
  no refit, and the `test_definitions` digest is re-pinned with that reason.
  - It lands after Phase B's backfill (B2), never before. Otherwise B2 would also
    rewrite `league_pct` on every row it touches, and its changed-row counts would no
    longer measure the O-line fix. That's slice 2.
- **Bare, said bare:**
  - defense tackles, pressures and the other PFR-derived rates: a median carries the
    same present-only inflation that gates the percentile;
  - coverage history;
  - environment (physical units);
  - market (the line is its own reference);
  - availability counts.
  - Each section with bare numbers says so in one line, from one section→reason map.
- **Cohort without the per-row string:**
  - the table caption names the cohort, built from the rows;
  - each row's position group and Last wk cell carry its own part;
  - teams get "of 32 teams, entering week N" in the header.

### B. Cutting the page
- **A player shows by default when the analyst ranked them in some family**, i.e. their
  headline `_pct` is non-null. The shown set and the ranked set are then the same
  players, by construction.
  - **Deviation from the scoping's wording, made at build time:** the scoping said
    "meets the per-game-played minimum", computed on the web. It uses the analyst's own
    result instead, so the web doesn't reimplement the gate.
  - Defense can't use it yet, because its headline `_pct` is gated null and the others
    aren't granted. So defense stays collapsed and unfiltered until slice 5.
- Hidden rows are counted, never silent: offensive line gets one collapsed line, and
  everyone else gets a "below the minimum" count with the full table inside.
- **Missing row ≠ NULL:**
  - a usage row with no efficiency row is "not in any family";
  - a NULL position group is "no position group";
  - each case has a test.
- **Open by default:** status, lines, environment, availability, the seven headline
  matchup rows, and role/passing/rushing/receiving filtered.
- **Collapsed:** arithmetic, the other 13 matchup rows, defense, coverage history,
  market detail and raw signals.

### C. Five ways to read a number
1. **Estimate vs observed.** A column-group header replaces per-column stars.
2. **Own % replaces "Stab".** Team stability (`w_cur + w_prior = 1 − w_league`) and the
   player headline stability are one definition: the share of the estimate that isn't
   the average filled in. Defense keeps its ‡ note. Team values still dim, players
   don't (user decision 2026-09-30).
3. **Pct means higher than X% of the named cohort, everywhere.** Direction-neutral.
   - A good/bad tint comes from one direction table, tested both ways against the
     signals shown (slice 2).
   - Defense columns say "allowed".
   - No "matchup edge" column: it implies an interaction effect P10 measured as absent.
4. **A sample always names its unit:** "L4 targets", never "L4 n".
5. **Time scope lives in the section header.**

Source tags (PFR/NGS/FTN) are attribution, not something to interpret.

### D. The honesty load (slice 4)
- **What moves:** per-column tooltips and legends go into one numbered notes panel per
  section, each note written once.
- **What stays in the row:** dashes, withheld markers, cohort marks, "not used by the
  model", "Not ranked", and the dimming.
- **What it costs:**
  - jump-to-read instead of hover;
  - a cropped column loses its note;
  - the biggest test rewrite in the plan, with a mutation check per rewritten test.
- **What it gains:** phones. `title` tooltips don't appear on touch screens at all.

### E. Tendency context (slice 6)
- **A new L2 Tendency analyst**, reading `player_game_pbp` (by `opponent_team`) and
  writing `signals`.
- **It needs:**
  - a migration (`signals_sector_check` has no `'tendency'`; don't reuse `'scheme'`,
    which P10 closed);
  - an `architecture.md` row, registry entries, a fixture and tests.
- **Signal values:**
  - `stability` is null (raw rates, and the page says so);
  - `league_pct` is filled.
- **Labels:** it includes garbage time, and blitz rate carries FTN's CC BY-SA statement.
  It's labeled tendency, never efficiency.
- About 2,100 rows/season. That's derived, not measured; measure it after the first
  write.
